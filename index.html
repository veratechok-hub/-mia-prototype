Compliance & Audit Posture

How records are protected, standardized, and made interoperable

PHI-safe by design

MIA processes but does not retain PHI after session completion. The network view holds de-identified, aggregated evidence only. BAA available upon request.

Active

Provider-signed before submission

Every record is reviewed and authorized by the care provider before it reaches a payer. No unreviewed output enters the claims workflow.

Enforced

Built to billing standards

Documentation follows CMS Community Health Worker guidance and state Medicaid doula program requirements — formatted for submission from the start.

Aligned

Interoperable output

Structured data designed to map to FHIR standards, enabling clean handoffs to EHRs, health-system workflows, and outcomes reporting layers.

FHIR-mappable

Encrypted storage & audit trail

Records are encrypted at rest and carry a timestamped milestone-clearance and sign-off history — every submitted claim is traceable and audit-ready.

Active
Co-develop the outcomes layer with PDI.
PDI is in active pilot with Oklahoma birth-worker organizations and is seeking a small number of MCO and health-system partners to define the measures that matter most to your members.
